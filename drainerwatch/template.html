        What we cannot see

        • No traces. Everything comes from logs and top-level transactions, so internal (factory) creations and native-coin internal splits are invisible and native drains are under-counted.
        • Only ERC-20 transfers are analysed; NFT transfers are ignored.
        • USD is known for stablecoins, wrapped and native coins only. Other tokens show the amount without a price, and appear as short pale ticks in the seismograph.
        • Operator and affiliate roles are inferred from share sizes; deposit-address links are a 72-hour forward heuristic with medium confidence.
        • Deployers are never guessed: when the creation is not visible and Etherscan has no answer the deployer is shown as unknown.
        • The scanner can fall behind the chain head; the block number and lag are shown at the top for each chain.
